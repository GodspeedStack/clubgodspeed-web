Common questions

Before you commit

What is the time commitment?

We expect players to train at least twice a week. Consistency is the only thing that produces real change.

Do you travel for tournaments?

Yes, once a group is ready. Players need exposure to competition outside their local bubble.

Is this for beginners?

The programs are built for serious athletes. Motivated beginners are welcome, but the intensity is high, so start with a session and see if it fits.
