Success

Every practice, every game, and every interaction within Godspeed is intentional. We expect effort, respect, and commitment from our players and our parents. In return, we will give your son the tools, coaching, and competitive environment to grow both on and off the court.

What We Expect

  • ✓ Show up on time, every time. Consistent attendance at practices and games is non-negotiable. Your son's teammates are counting on him.
  • ✓ Respect coaches, referees, and opponents. We teach our players to compete hard and lose with dignity. We expect parents to model the same.
  • ✓ Communicate proactively. If your son will miss practice, text Coach Scott or Coach Gene at least 2 hours before. If there is a schedule conflict, injury, or family situation that will affect multiple sessions, let us know as soon as you are aware so we can plan accordingly. We work with families, not against them.
  • ✓ Support the brotherhood. Cheer for every player. Celebrate every teammate's win. This is a team, not a showcase.
Zero-Tolerance Policy Godspeed has zero tolerance for the following from players or parents: verbal abuse of coaches, referees, or other parents; sideline coaching that contradicts staff; negative talk about teammates; and failure to uphold financial commitments without prior communication. Violations may result in immediate suspension or removal from the program with no refund. We protect this culture for every family in the program.

Help Us Build This

Godspeed is not a drop-off program. We need parents who want to be part of something special. Volunteer to help at tournaments. Share ideas that strengthen our community. The best programs are built by families who invest more than money.

Practice Commitment

Signature Required

Practice Commitment

Godspeed Basketball practices are designed to be high-intensity, competitive training sessions. By signing this agreement, I acknowledge and agree to the following:

Attendance Expectations

Regular attendance at practices is expected. Every session matters because when one player is absent, the entire team has to adjust drills, re-pair groups, and change plans on the fly. Showing up consistently is how we build trust, chemistry, and habits as a team.

If your son will miss practice for any reason, please text Coach Scott or Coach Gene before practice so we can plan accordingly. A quick message is all we need. We work with families and understand things come up. The only thing we ask is that you communicate.

Physical Demands

Training sessions include sprinting, agility drills, strength conditioning, and full-court gameplay. I acknowledge that these activities are physically demanding and confirm that my child is physically able to participate. I will notify coaches immediately if my child has any physical limitation that could affect participation.

Equipment

Players are expected to arrive with proper basketball shoes, a water bottle, and appropriate athletic clothing. Godspeed Basketball is not responsible for lost or damaged personal property.

Parental Code of Conduct

Signature Required

Parent and Guardian Expectations

Godspeed Basketball is built on mutual respect between coaches, players, and families. Your behavior as a parent directly shapes your child's experience and the culture of this program. By signing this agreement, you commit to the following:

Sideline Conduct

  • I will encourage my child and all players positively at all times during games and practices.
  • I will not coach from the sideline, yell instructions to players, or contradict coaching staff during games or practices.
  • I will respect all game officials. I understand that referees are human and disagreements with calls are never an excuse for verbal abuse.
  • I will not engage in negative talk about other players, parents, or coaches within earshot of any players.

Communication Standards

  • I will direct all concerns about coaching decisions, playing time, or strategy to the coaching staff privately, never during or immediately after a game.
  • I will observe a 24-hour cooling-off period before raising concerns after a game.
  • I will communicate schedule conflicts or issues in advance through proper channels.

Financial Responsibility

  • I will meet all financial obligations on time as outlined in the season dues agreement.
  • If I experience financial hardship, I will communicate proactively with the program director rather than simply missing payments.

Consequences

Violations of this code of conduct will result in the following escalation: first offense is a verbal warning, second offense is a written warning, and third offense is suspension or removal from the program. Severe violations such as physical threats, racist or discriminatory language, or dangerous behavior result in immediate removal with no prior warning required.
