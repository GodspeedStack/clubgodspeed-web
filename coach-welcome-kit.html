Your first two weeks at Godspeed

Everything a new coach needs to walk into the gym ready. Read it once, keep it on your phone.

What we are building

Godspeed develops young athletes, ages 10 to 15, through three words: Brotherhood. Habits. Success. Wins come from habits. Habits come from how we run every practice. You are here to teach habits first and basketball second.

Week one

DayDo this
Day 1Finish setup in the portal (you did this). Read the Code of Conduct again. Text Coach Scott your first practice date.
Day 2Open your team in the portal. Learn every player's name and jersey number before you meet them.
Day 3Check each athlete's medical notes and emergency contact in the portal. Know who has an inhaler or an allergy.
First practiceArrive 15 minutes early. Walk the gym. Find the AED and the exits. Start on time, end on time.
After practiceGrade every player in the portal within 24 hours: Focus, Hustle, Skill, IQ. Parents see it.

Week two

  • Run one full practice on your own with a director watching.
  • Finish the free concussion course (CDC HEADS UP) and send the certificate to a director.
  • Introduce yourself to every parent in person, one sentence each: who you are, what you coach.

Where and when

Team practiceMonday, Tuesday, Thursday. Doors at 5:00 pm, practice 6:00 to 8:00 pm. Denver Public Schools gym, CGK8 main gym.
Small group and 1 on 1Wednesday and Friday, one hour, outdoors or at a rec center. Set by Coach Scott.
Gym rulesNo outside shoes on the floor. Water only on the floor. We leave the gym cleaner than we found it. Custodian time is paid by the hour, so we are out by 8:00 sharp.
WeatherOutdoor sessions move to the rec center. Directors send the call by 2:00 pm.

How grading works

After every practice and game, each player gets four scores from 1 to 10: Focus, Hustle, Skill, IQ. Be honest and consistent. A 7 means solid. A 10 is rare. Add one sentence of notes when something stood out, good or bad. Grades roll into each player's report, and parents read them the same night, so write what you would say to their face.

Who to contact

Anything about a player or a parentCoach Scott (director). Text first, call if urgent.
Practice plans and drillsCoach Gene (co-coach).
Injury or safety911 if serious, then the parent, then a director. Same day.
Portal problemsCoach Scott. Send a screenshot.

Director phone numbers are in the Coach Portal under Staff. They are not printed here.

Three rules that never bend

  • Two adults, always. Never alone with one athlete in private.
  • When in doubt, sit them out. Any head hit, they are done for the day.
  • No money, no gear, no DMs. Families pay through the portal. You do not message athletes privately.
