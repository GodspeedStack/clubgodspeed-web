A different kind of coach.

For a different kind of player. Three coaches, one standard, and a program built around what a boy becomes rather than what he wins this season.

Coach True

Player development

Training philosophy

What the program is built on

01
Movement science
Principles for efficiency, speed and injury prevention, tailored to basketball movement.
02
Modern IQ
Decision-making for the positionless game, so a player adapts instead of memorising.
03
Psychology
Confidence built the way performance coaches build it, not by praise alone.
04
Identity
Purpose-driven mentorship founded on character and personal responsibility.
