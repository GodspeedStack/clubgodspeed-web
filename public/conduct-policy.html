TEAM STANDARD

Godspeed Basketball is building a program that competes at a national level against the best teams in the country. That standard starts with how our players carry themselves on and off the court. Every player and family must read, understand, and agree to this policy before participating in any Godspeed practice, training, or game.

Our Standard

We represent something bigger than any single game. Brotherhood means holding each other accountable, not tearing each other down. Every player who wears a Godspeed jersey is expected to compete with intensity, discipline, and respect for teammates, opponents, coaches, and officials at all times.

Fighting and Physical Altercations

Any physical fight, whether on the court or off the court, at a practice, game, tournament, or team event, will result in an immediate 1 to 3 game suspension and a formal investigation by the coaching staff.

The suspended player must continue attending all practices and games during the suspension and investigation period. Suspension means no playing time, not absence from the team.

Altercations Between Godspeed Players

If a fight or act of taunting occurs between two Godspeed players, both players will be suspended for a minimum of 1 game regardless of who started it. Both players must attend all practices and games during the investigation.

If the investigation determines that further action is needed, the player or players involved will be excluded from the team indefinitely, with no refund of any dues, fees, or payments made.

Taunting and Unsportsmanlike Conduct

Taunting opponents or teammates, trash talk that crosses the line, or any behavior that embarrasses the program will be treated with the same seriousness as a physical altercation. The coaching staff determines when that line has been crossed.

CONSEQUENCES AT A GLANCE
Fighting (any context) 1 to 3 game suspension + investigation. Must attend all team activities.
Fight or taunting between Godspeed players Both players suspended 1 game minimum + investigation.
Investigation finds further action needed Indefinite exclusion from the team. No refund.
